# Govern

> Administer users, access control, organizational structure, and compliance policies for your LangSmith organization.

Administer your LangSmith organization: manage users and access control, organize workspaces and applications, and configure policies and compliance.

Governance also extends to what agents do at runtime: [spend and data protection policies](../../LLM%20Gateway/Governance/Spend%20policies.md) on model calls, [sandboxed execution](../../Deploy/Sandboxes/LangSmith%20Sandboxes.md) for agent code, and [human approval](../../Deploy/Agent%20Server/Capabilities/Human-in-the-loop%20using%20server%20API.md) before sensitive actions.
